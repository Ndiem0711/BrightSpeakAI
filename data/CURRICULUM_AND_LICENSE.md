# Lesson dataset — original, not copied from textbooks

- **125 original short practice prompts**, 25 per grade (1–5), five topical units per grade.
- Topics are *thematically aligned* with Vietnam's 2018 general education English curriculum and publicly described Global Success units. This is **not** an official textbook transcription or official Global Success dataset.
- **No copyrighted textbook pages, audio, exercises, illustrations or scanned books are included.** Licensing is not granted by the authors/publishers. To use their exact materials, obtain a license from Vietnam Education Publishing House and authorized distributors.
- For grades 1–2, English classes depend on the applicable curriculum and school implementation. Teachers should verify actual learning objectives.
- Source references: Bộ GD&ĐT Circular 32/2018/TT-BGDĐT; [official educational materials](https://hanhtrangso.nxbgd.vn) and [Global Success resources](https://sachmem.vn).
- All model texts above were newly authored for the project; they require pedagogical review before use with real students.
